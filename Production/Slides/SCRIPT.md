# Shovel pitch: talk track (about 2 min; the demo video is submitted separately)

Keys: arrows or space to move, `f` fullscreen, `t` elapsed time.

1. **Hook (10 s).** "This is Shovel. Drop in a bill of quantities and get it back marked up in red pen."
2. **Problem (25 s).** "The Rechnungshof Baden-Württemberg looked at 48 public projects. One order for inspection hatches started at 50,000 euros and ended near 600,000 after five change orders. Gaps in the LV open that door, and nobody has time to read 3,000 items twice."
3. **The document (20 s).** "This is a real tender from your dataset. Short text says DN 125, long text says DN 100. Nobody planted that. Shovel underlines it and drafts the question to the client."
4. **The letter (20 s).** "One click per note: ask the client, price it in, or keep it as a change-order chance. The letter is written, with the surcharges, the bid draft and the list for the site manager beside it."
5. **What changed (20 s).** "When the client sends a revised LV days before the deadline, Shovel shows every changed quantity and text and the new risks. The seven changes in this example are ours."
6. **Proof (20 s).** "All 43 of your files read, 4,239 items checked. Where the same LV exists in two formats, six pairs, we get identical quantities every time."
7. **Close (15 s).** "The estimator decides. Rules you can trust, prices from your own projects, AI only where it can quote the tender word for word."
